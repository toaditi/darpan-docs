Tell us what Darpan should do next. Submit an idea and vote requests up or down — no account needed. The most-wanted requests feed the [roadmap](/releases/roadmap).

<div id="feature-forum">
  <p className="ff-fallback">The request board needs JavaScript enabled to load.</p>
</div>

Every request is open for voting and discussion for 14 days — then it either joins the [roadmap](/releases/roadmap) or closes. One vote per person per request; statuses are set by the Darpan team, and shipped requests move to [Release updates](/releases/updates).
